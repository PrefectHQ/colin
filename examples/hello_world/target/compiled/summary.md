# Summary Example

Hello! Welcome to Colin.

This is a simple document that other documents can reference.



## Extracted Info

The main message is: "Hello! Welcome to Colin."

## LLM-Generated Content

Here's a haiku about being welcomed:

Doors open with warmth
Gentle voices call me in
Home found in kind words

This haiku captures the essence of welcome - the opening gesture (doors), the warm reception (gentle voices), and the emotional comfort that comes from being genuinely welcomed (finding home in kindness).

## With Explicit Model

The translation to French would be:

**Bonjour ! Bienvenue chez Colin.**

**Ceci est un document simple que d'autres documents peuvent référencer.**

Note: "Colin" is kept as is since it's a proper name. The translation of "Welcome to Colin" uses "Bienvenue chez Colin" which is the most natural way to express this in French, though depending on context, "Bienvenue sur Colin" could also be used if Colin refers to a platform or website.
